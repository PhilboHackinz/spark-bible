sherpa-onnx and ONNX Runtime

Spark Bible can use an optional Kokoro voice. The speech library is
sherpa-onnx 1.12.15 (https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx), including
the Kotlin classes under com.k2fsa.sherpa.onnx.

https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

The Kokoro model (kokoro-en-v0_19) is not inside the app. It downloads
only when that voice is chosen, from the sherpa-onnx tts-models release.
