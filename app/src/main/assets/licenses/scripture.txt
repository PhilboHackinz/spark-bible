Spark Bible includes these Bible translations on the phone. None of them need a connection.

Public domain:

- World English Bible
- King James Version
- American Standard Version
- Geneva Bible (1599)
- Douay-Rheims American Edition (1899)
- Berean Standard Bible, dedicated to the public domain on April 30, 2023

The older verse files were prepared from the public-domain texts published by
midvash/bible-data (https://github.com/midvash/bible-data).
