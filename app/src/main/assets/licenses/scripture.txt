Spark Bible includes five public-domain Bible translations:

- World English Bible
- King James Version
- American Standard Version
- Geneva Bible (1599)
- Douay-Rheims American Edition (1899)

The verse files were prepared from the public-domain texts published by
midvash/bible-data (https://github.com/midvash/bible-data).

These scripture texts are not covered by the app's code license.
They are free to use in the United States.
